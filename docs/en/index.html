WITHOUT THE COMPLICATIONS

Answers to important questions.

Is this an extra payment?

No. FR0512 allocates part of your income tax; it does not debit an additional donation from your bank account. A direct bank transfer in the support section is a separate way to give.

Who can use the GPM scheme?

The scheme concerns Lithuanian personal income tax and VMI eligibility conditions. Your website language, citizenship or residence abroad alone does not establish eligibility. Check your own tax situation with VMI.

Can I support several recipients?

Yes. The total allocation for recipients in this category must not exceed 1.2%. How you divide that share is your choice.

I previously chose several years. Must I apply again?

Check your existing allocations in EDS. How older applications will operate after religious communities regain eligibility in 2027 is still being clarified; we do not promise automatic renewal.

Do I need to file GPM311?

If you are required to declare income, file by the deadline set by VMI. FR0512 does not replace the income tax return. Failure to meet declaration requirements may prevent the support from being transferred.

Will this website send my tax details to the parish?

No. There is no tax-data submission form here. Applications are made only in EDS. Calculator values stay in your browser.

How can I help without GPM?

You can make a voluntary bank transfer using the details in the parish-support section. Contact the parish about donating materials or helping with work.
